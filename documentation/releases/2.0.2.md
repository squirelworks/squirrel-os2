# 2.0.2 — 2.0 clean-up, part two

- Date: 2026-11-04
- TODO.md tasks: **2.0.2-subnet**, **2.0.2-devunreg**, **2.0.2-aging**, **2.0.2-scroll** (the 2.0.x clean-up in `ROADMAP.md`).
- Changes:
  - `ifconfig <ip> <mask> <gw>` refuses a gateway that is not on the new address's subnet (`(gw XOR ip) AND mask` is not zero). It prints "Gateway is not on that subnet", localized in en, la and tlh, and leaves the address, mask and gateway as they were. Before, it accepted the gateway, and every packet for another network then went nowhere.
  - `SYS_DEVUNREG` (34, `ebx` = name) removes a device that `SYS_DEVREG` made and frees its slot. Before, slots were never given back, so `run drvtest` failed the second time in a boot. A removed slot keeps its table index, so other handles do not move. Its old handle is refused, `devices` skips it, and the next registration reuses it. Built-in devices cannot be removed. `drvtest` now registers, uses and removes `null`, and passes every time.
  - Priority aging: a READY process that has waited `AGE_TICKS` (two seconds) since it last ran counts as above every priority for one slice, so a priority-0 process can no longer starve beside a busy higher priority. `priotest` still passes. The new `run agetest` prints `age: PASS` (`kernel/processes.md`).
  - Lists scroll. A list with more items than fit in its window shows the rows that fit, scrolled to keep the selection shown. Before, it was not drawn at all. `wg_list_item` maps a row as drawn to its item, and the desktop's mouse click uses it, so a click on a scrolled list picks the item under the pointer. The new `scrolltest` prints `scroll: PASS` (`kernel/widgets.md`).
  - Banner `2.0.2`.
  - Regression checks: `ifconfig-offnet`, `ifconfig-offnet-keep`, `ifconfig-offnet-la` `drv-devices` (now: `null` is gone afterwards), `drv-again` (six runs in one boot), `age`, `scrolltest` (plus `ver-banner`, `lang-banner`).
- Known limitations:
  - A list scrolls only to follow the keyboard selection; there is no scroll bar or wheel yet.
- Status: implemented
