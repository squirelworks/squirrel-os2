# 1.9.5 — Ethernet and ARP

- Date: 2026-11-01
- TODO.md task: **2.0-arp**.
- Changes:
  - **Ethernet framing and ARP** (`kernel/arp.asm`, `kernel/network.md`). The guest address is 10.0.2.15. A request is a broadcast frame; replies fill a table of eight addresses. A request for our own address is answered.
  - **`arp <ip>`** prints the Ethernet address, `No reply` when nothing answers within two seconds, or `Bad address`.
  - Banner `1.9.5`.
  - Regression checks: `arp-self`, `arp-gw`, `arp-none`, `arp-bad`, `arp-nonic`.
- Known limitations:
  - No IPv4 yet (`2.0-ping`). Frames that are not ARP are dropped.
  - The address, and the two-second wait, are fixed. The table holds eight rows.
  - One card, at I/O 0x300. Tested in QEMU only.
- Status: implemented
- Design: `kernel/network.md`
