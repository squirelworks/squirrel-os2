# 1.9.8 — A small TCP client

- Date: 2026-11-01
- TODO.md task: **2.0-tcp**.
- Changes:
  - **One TCP connection** (`kernel/tcp.asm`, `kernel/network.md`). Connect, send, receive and close. Incoming options are skipped. Data is kept only in order. A segment is sent again once if nothing comes back within a second.
  - **`fetch <ip> <port> <path>`** sends `GET <path> HTTP/1.0` and prints the reply. `No reply` when the handshake or the request is not acknowledged.
  - **`tools/fileserve.py`** answers one HTTP/1.0 request on 127.0.0.1. The guest reaches it at 10.0.2.2, QEMU's user-network gateway.
  - The NE2000 byte count includes the 4-byte receive header and the frame, not a CRC. Counting a CRC as well made a short IP packet look truncated.
  - Banner `1.9.8`.
  - Regression check: `fetch-hello`.
- Known limitations:
  - One connection, and only the client side. No urgent data and no out-of-order queue.
  - The address, mask and gateway are still fixed. Tested in QEMU only.
- Status: implemented
- Design: `kernel/network.md`
