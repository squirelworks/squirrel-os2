# 1.9.6 — IPv4 and ping

- Date: 2026-11-01
- TODO.md task: **2.0-ping**.
- Changes:
  - **IPv4** (`kernel/ip.asm`, `kernel/network.md`). Twenty-byte headers only: no options, and a fragment is dropped. The guest is 10.0.2.15/24, gateway 10.0.2.2. 127.0.0.0/8 and the guest's own address go over loopback; other local addresses go out the card after ARP; everything else goes to the gateway.
  - **ICMP echo.** A request to us is answered. `ping <ip>` sends four requests and prints how many replies came back, as `4 of 4`.
  - Banner `1.9.6`.
  - Regression checks: `ping-lo`, `ping-gw`, `ping-bad`.
- Known limitations:
  - No UDP yet (`2.0-udp`). One ping at a time, and each reply has one second to arrive.
  - The address, mask and gateway are fixed. Tested in QEMU only.
- Status: implemented
- Design: `kernel/network.md`
