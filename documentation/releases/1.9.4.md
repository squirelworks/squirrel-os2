# 1.9.4 — NE2000 network card

- Date: 2026-11-01
- TODO.md task: **2.0-nic**.
- Changes:
  - **An NE2000 driver** (`kernel/ne2k.asm`, `kernel/network.md`) for the ISA card at I/O 0x300 that QEMU offers as `ne2k_isa`. It is polled: port I/O only, no IRQ handler and no bus mastering. The card is the interface `ne0` behind `net_send` and `net_recv`; `ne_poll` moves what the card has received onto its queue.
  - **`devices`** lists the card as `ne0  net  ready` with its MAC address, or `absent` when there is no card.
  - **`nettest`**, with a card, also sends an ARP request for 10.0.2.2 and waits up to two seconds for a reply: `nic: PASS`.
  - **`tools/qemu_screen.py --nic`** attaches the card on QEMU's user network.
  - Banner `1.9.4`.
  - Regression checks: `nic-absent`, `nic-devices`, `nic-test`.
- Known limitations:
  - No protocols yet: the ARP in `nettest` is a fixed frame, and ARP proper is `2.0-arp`.
  - Frames are only taken off the card when something polls; a full ring drops what comes next.
  - One card, at I/O 0x300 only. Tested in QEMU only.
- Status: implemented
- Design: `kernel/network.md`
